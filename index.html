Every match goes through Sydney

Online games like League of Legends and Valorant run on servers in Sydney, so every Australian's game data has to travel there and back. "Ping" is the time that round trip takes, in milliseconds (ms). The further from Sydney you live, the higher your ping, and in fast games even a few milliseconds can decide who wins.
